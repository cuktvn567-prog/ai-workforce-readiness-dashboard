---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the potential for AI adoption across different occupations.
- Evaluate AI Capability and Worker Readiness.
- Measure the gap between AI capability and workforce readiness.
- Identify occupations with high transformation risks.
- Develop a decision-support framework for AI investment prioritization.
- Provide strategic recommendations for workforce transformation.

---

## 🔎 Business Questions

The project focuses on questions such as:

- Which occupations have the highest potential for AI automation?
- How ready are workers to work with AI?
- Where is the largest gap between AI capability and workforce readiness?
- Which occupations require reskilling before AI deployment?
- Which occupations should prioritize AI investment?
- How would transformation strategies change under different development scenarios?
